# Research planning capability

For ordinary project research advice, first restore project-owned observations,
baseline history and current scope through the resolved installed lifecycle.
Use its `references/research-strategy.md` and planning template to state the mode
reason, error breakdown, competing hypotheses, contrasts, variation/tuning policy,
total trial cost and result-dependent next choices. Metric improvement and useful
discriminating evidence are separate outcomes. Missing or failed measurements
cannot establish a model ceiling. Keep project thresholds configurable and
preserve restrictions on model changes and current execution contracts.
The draft is an advisory study record; it is not formal step completion, promotion
or permission to dispatch trials. Use the governed route below only when requested.

Resolve the installed `sdm-lifecycle` Skill, resume the exact workspace, and
advance only lifecycle steps 1-2 or step 21 successor framing. S01 binds the
Product Requirement/JIRA identity, owner, impact, constraints, and non-goals.
S03 binds Product Brief, PRD, and Architecture as exact immutable
`{kind, uri, digest, revision}` BMAD references and binds the exact S01 digest.
S02 binds that S01/S03 closure plus history sources, question, falsifiable hypothesis,
mechanism, uncertainty, alternatives, and downstream evidence. Avoid TARGET-as-
MUST language and post-result reframing. A successor cites the prior D05 and
failure evidence with reciprocal lineage but never edits its parent.
