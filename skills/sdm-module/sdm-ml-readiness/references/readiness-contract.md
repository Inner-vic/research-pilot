# Readiness contract

S04 requires linked Product Requirement/JIRA, S01/S02, applicable G01-G13 and
B01-B03, an isolated treatment, deterministic engineering acceptance, and the
three named planning outputs. E02 additionally requires a canonical E01
candidate digest, immutable dataset/split/evaluator/budget identities, current
baseline reproduction and contract-preflight S13 receipts, and independent
readiness authority. S09 is later and requires the merged revision plus passing
geometry, tensor, finite, tiny-set, checkpoint-resume, and missing-modality
preflight receipts. Emit the exact artifact IDs and digests checked, every
missing prerequisite, the governing rule digest, and the affected gate.

Data operations are limited to `data.quality-check`; CI operations are limited
to `jenkins.contract-preflight` and `jenkins.post-implementation-preflight` as
applicable. The outer Agent activates configured Skills; Python never executes
their binding names.
